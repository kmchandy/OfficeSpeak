# inbox_triage — Pat's spec

Pat's English description: a non-coder saying what they want — sources,
processing, output — with no topology, agent count, or framework details.

---

Watch my Gmail inbox for unread messages (up to 20 at a time). Classify each
email by urgency: urgent, normal, or low. Show me the urgent ones in the
terminal, archive the normal ones to a JSONL file, and drop the low-priority
ones.
