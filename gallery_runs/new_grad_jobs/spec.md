# new_grad_jobs — Pat's spec

Pat's English description: a non-coder saying what they want — sources,
processing, output — with no topology, agent count, or framework details.

---

I want to keep an eye on new-grad and entry-level tech jobs. Watch Hacker News'
hiring postings. For each posting, decide whether it's a good fit for someone
just starting out — an entry-level, new-grad, junior, or intern role in software
engineering, AI/ML, or data science, based in the US or Canada or fully remote,
and prefer ones that mention Python. For the postings that fit, rewrite each one
into a clean, scannable brief with the role title, company, location, type, key
technologies, a two-sentence summary, any notable flags (like visa sponsorship
or remote-friendly), and a link. Show the matching briefs in my terminal and
save them to a JSONL file. Also record the postings that didn't fit in that same
file, so I can audit later what got skipped.
